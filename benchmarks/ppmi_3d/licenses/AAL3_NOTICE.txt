AAL3 atlas notice (STEWARD D-037; written 2026-11-20 for prompt P1b)

Atlas: AAL3v1 (Automated Anatomical Labelling atlas 3), 1 mm label volume AAL3v1_1mm.nii.gz and label table
AAL3v1_1mm.nii.txt, taken from the official package AAL3v2_for_SPM12.tar.gz of the Neurofunctional Imaging
Group (GIN-IMN, Bordeaux): https://www.gin.cnrs.fr/en/tools/aal/ (downloaded 2026-11-20; SHA-256 in
analysis/agmd_atlas/SHA256SUMS.txt).

Terms of use, as stated by the provider:
- AAL3 User Guide (AAL3_UserGuide_April2024.pdf, page 1): AAL3 "is available to the scientific community as
  copyright freeware under the terms of the GNU General Public License."
- The same page says AAL 3v2 (5 April 2024) only adds the operating licence "GNU General Public Licence" in the
  readme, and that the atlas files themselves are unchanged and still named AAL3v1.
- The downloaded archive does not contain that readme; the GIN page states GNU GPL in a readme that the
  archive does not contain. The user guide is therefore the only licence statement we have read.

Required citation (user guide, page 5, "References"):
  Rolls, E.T., Huang, C.C., Lin, C.-P., Feng, J., Joliot, M., 2020. Automated anatomical labelling atlas 3.
  NeuroImage 206:116189. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.116189
Original references listed by the user guide (page 5): Tzourio-Mazoyer et al., NeuroImage 15:273-289 (2002);
Rolls, Joliot & Tzourio-Mazoyer, NeuroImage 122:1-5 (2015).

Atlas files are not redistributed with this code. data/atlas/*.nii.gz is listed in .gitignore; users must
download AAL3 from the official GIN page and rebuild the resampled atlas (see data/atlas/README.md).
